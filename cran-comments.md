## Submission

This is an update from 0.4.0 to 0.5.0.

Visual defaults change on purpose. The default intensity is now 0.5,
specular highlights are static, and dark glass uses a short top sheen.
NEWS.md documents those changes. `vignette("migrating")` shows how to
approximate the 0.4.0 look.
